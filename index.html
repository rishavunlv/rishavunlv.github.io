What The Platform Offers

The platform is designed for security leaders, operations teams, and business stakeholders who need faster, evidence-based decisions. Each page focuses on a distinct use case while maintaining a shared model for risk, cost, and strategic action.

Core Modules

  • CISO Budget Defense: compare cold vs hot site economics
  • GraphRAG Explorer: visualize risk propagation and attack paths
  • Small Business Reality Check: calculate ALE and ROSI quickly
  • Cybersecurity Risk Assessment: governance and posture analytics
  • Cross-page navigation for a single executive narrative
